A. One grasp, all the way through. A hand reaches for a cup. One attempt travels a diagram that grows one part per beat: reflex, verifier, friction cone, pass or reject, planner, ledger, back into the reflex. Then many attempts at speed show why the gate can be loose: every path pays back, as an answer or as training data.

B. Two machines, same day. A split screen. On one side every motion goes to the big planner; on the other the reflex goes first. Not built: it invites cost claims the public copy must not make.

C. The ledger's point of view. Told from the append-only ledger as rows arrive. Not built: abstract until the viewer has seen one attempt.
